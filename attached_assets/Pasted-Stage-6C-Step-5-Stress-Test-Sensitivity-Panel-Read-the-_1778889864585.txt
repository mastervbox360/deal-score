SCENARIO DEFINITIONS

Base Case — current inputs as entered
Rent -10% — gross monthly income reduced by 10%, all other inputs unchanged
Rate +1.5% — mortgage interest rate increased by 1.5%, all other inputs unchanged

For each scenario, recalculate:

Monthly Cash Flow — net monthly income after mortgage and expenses
Cash-on-Cash ROI — annual net cash flow divided by total cash invested, expressed as a percentage

These are derived calculations only — do not add any new input fields.
