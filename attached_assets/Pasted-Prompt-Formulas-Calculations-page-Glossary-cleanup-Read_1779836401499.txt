BTL, HMO, SA, SOCIAL, BRRR — Left column
Section: Yield & Return

Gross Yield — Annual rent as a percentage of purchase price — (Annual Rent ÷ Purchase Price) × 100
Net Yield — Income after operating costs divided by purchase price. Mortgage excluded (UK standard) — (Annual Income − Operating Costs) ÷ Purchase Price × 100
Cash-on-Cash ROI — Annual cash flow as a percentage of total cash invested. Includes mortgage — (Annual Cash Flow ÷ Cash Invested) × 100
Payback Period — Years to recover invested capital from annual cash flow — Cash Invested ÷ Annual Cash Flow
Equity on Day One (omit for BRRR) — Equity when purchased below market value — Market Value − Purchase Price
Equity Created (BRRR only) — Equity generated through refurbishment — Post-Refurb Value − Total Cost In
BRRR Gross Yield (BRRR only) — Yield calculated on GDV — (Annual Rent ÷ Post-Refurb Value) × 100

Section: Cash Flow & Expenses (add marginTop: 10 to section header)

Effective Rent — Gross rent after void allowance deducted — Gross Rent × (1 − Void %)
Net Operating Income — Effective rent minus all operating costs, excluding mortgage — Effective Rent − Operating Costs
Net Cash Flow — Monthly surplus after all costs including mortgage — Net Operating Income − Mortgage Payment
Monthly Mortgage (Interest-Only) — Monthly interest payment — (Mortgage Amount × Annual Rate %) ÷ 12

BTL, HMO, SA, SOCIAL, BRRR — Right column
Section: Stress Testing & Resilience

Break-Even Rent — Minimum rent to cover all costs at current rate — Operating Costs + Mortgage Payment
Rent Headroom — Buffer before deal becomes cash-flow negative — Contracted Rent − Break-Even Rent
Break-Even Rate — Mortgage rate at which cash flow reaches zero — Solved from: Rent = Operating Costs + (Mortgage Amount × Rate ÷ 12)
Sensitivity Analysis — Performance under rent −10% and rate +1.5% applied independently — See sensitivity table in report

Section: Capital & Structure (add marginTop: 10 to section header)

Cash Invested — Total capital deployed into the deal — Deposit + Tax + Refurb + Other Costs
LTV — Loan to value ratio — (Mortgage Amount ÷ Property Value) × 100
Refinance Loan (BRRR only) — Capital released on refinance — Post-Refurb Value × (Refinance % ÷ 100)
Cash Left In (BRRR only) — Capital remaining after refinance — Total Cost In − Refinance Loan

Section: HMO (HMO only — add marginTop: 10 to section header)

Profit Per Room — Monthly cash flow divided by number of rooms — Monthly Cash Flow ÷ Number of Rooms
Gross Monthly Rent — Total room income at current occupancy — Rent per Room × Rooms × (Occupancy % ÷ 100)


FLIP — Left column
Section: Project Returns

Total ROI — Net profit as a percentage of total cost — (Net Profit ÷ Total Cost In) × 100
Annualised ROI — ROI scaled to a 12-month equivalent — (Total ROI ÷ Project Length in months) × 12
Profit on Cost — Net profit as a percentage of total project cost — (Net Profit ÷ Total Cost) × 100
Net Profit — Sale proceeds minus all costs — Expected Sale Price − Total Cost − Selling Costs
