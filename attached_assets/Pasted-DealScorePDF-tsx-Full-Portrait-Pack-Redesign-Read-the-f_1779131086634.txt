CONTEXT & RULES
These rules are non-negotiable throughout every change:

Brand/accent colours apply only to structural elements: borders, rules, top border accents on cards, bullet markers, section label text. Never on metric values or data text.
All metric values, financial figures, and data text must render in #1E2B3C (dark navy) or #1B3A6B. Never in brand colour.
readableBrand is the correct variable for section labels and structural accents on white backgrounds.
coverBg (darkened brand) or fixed #1B3A6B must be used for any dark panel headers — never raw brand or readableBrand as a background for table headers, as light brand colours can render as white-on-white.
Do not change: cover pages, photo pages, legal page, calculation logic, formula functions, props interface, helper functions, or the base StyleSheet. Only change page layout and visual presentation.
Do not add any new props to the interface.
