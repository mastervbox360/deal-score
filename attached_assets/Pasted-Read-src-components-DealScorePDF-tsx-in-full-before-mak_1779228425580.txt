Left column:

  Section 1 — "Yield & Return":
    Gross Yield — Annual rent divided by purchase price. Formula: (Annual Rent / Purchase Price) × 100
    Net Yield — Annual income minus operating costs divided by purchase price. Mortgage excluded (UK standard).
    Cash-on-Cash ROI — Annual cash flow divided by total cash invested. Includes mortgage payment. Formula: (Annual Cash Flow / Cash Invested) × 100
    Payback Period — Years to recover invested capital from cash flow. Formula: Cash Invested / Annual Cash Flow
    Equity on Day One — Immediate equity if property purchased below market value. Formula: Market Value − Purchase Price
    BMV — Below Market Value. The discount between purchase price and estimated market value.

  Section 2 — "Cash Flow & Expenses" (marginTop 10):
    Gross Rent — Total rental income before any deductions or void allowance.
    Void Allowance — Estimated cost of vacancy — periods with no rental income.
    Effective Rent — Gross rent after void allowance deducted. Formula: Gross Rent × (1 − Void %)
    Net Operating Income (NOI) — Effective rent minus all operating costs, excluding mortgage.
    Net Cash Flow — Monthly surplus after all costs including mortgage. Formula: NOI − Mortgage Payment
    Break-Even Rent — Minimum rent to cover all costs at the current mortgage rate.
    Rent Headroom — Buffer between contracted rent and break-even rent. Formula: Gross Rent − Break-Even Rent

Right column:

  Section 3 — "Stress Testing & Resilience":
    Break-Even Rent — The rent level at which monthly cash flow reaches exactly zero.
    Rent Headroom — How far rent can fall before the deal becomes cash-flow negative.
    Stress Test — Analysis of deal performance under higher mortgage rates and lower rent scenarios.
    Break-Even Rate — The mortgage rate at which monthly cash flow reaches zero.
    Sensitivity Analysis — Impact on returns of a 10% rent reduction and a 1.5% rate increase applied independently.

  Section 4 — "Mortgage, Tax & Structure" (marginTop 10):
    Interest-Only Mortgage — Monthly payment covers interest only. Capital balance remains unchanged throughout the term.
    LTV — Loan to Value. Mortgage amount expressed as a percentage of property value. Formula: Mortgage / Property Value × 100
    LTT / SDLT — Land Transaction Tax (Wales) or Stamp Duty Land Tax (England & NI). Government purchase tax on property transactions.
    Cash Invested — Total capital deployed into the deal. Formula: Deposit + Tax + Refurb + Other Costs
    Deal Score — Weighted composite score from 1 to 10 across six dimensions of deal quality.
    Sourcing Fee — Fee payable to the deal sourcer on legal completion.
    Cash-on-Cash ROI — The annual return on actual cash invested, after all costs including mortgage.
