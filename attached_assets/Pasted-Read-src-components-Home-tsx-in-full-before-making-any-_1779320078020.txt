STEP 3 — BACK-CALCULATION LOGIC
────────────────────────────────────────

All calculations use the existing cost inputs (refurb, other costs, SDLT, deposit %, mortgage rate, monthly expenses etc.) already entered by the user. Only purchase price is the unknown being solved for.

Use these formulas per strategy. Solve for maxPurchasePrice:

BTL / HMO / SOCIAL:
  From ROI constraint:
    annualProfit = targetROI/100 × cashInvested
    cashInvested = depositPct/100 × maxPrice + SDLT(maxPrice) + refurb + otherCosts + leaseExtensionCost + buyersPremium
    annualProfit = annualRent - annualOpCosts - annualMortgage(maxPrice)
    Solve iteratively: start at currentPurchasePrice, step down £1,000 until ROI ≥ targetROI
  From CF constraint:
    monthlyCF = monthlyIncome - monthlyMortgage(maxPrice) - monthlyOpCosts ≥ targetMonthlyCF
    Solve iteratively in same way
  maxPurchasePrice = minimum of the two constraints (whichever is binding)
  
  For HMO also solve from yield constraint:
    maxPriceFromYield = annualRent / (targetGrossYield/100)
    maxPurchasePrice = minimum of all three constraints

FLIP:
  maxPrice = GDV - (targetMarginPct/100 × totalCosts) - refurb - sellingCosts - otherCosts - buyersPremium
  Where totalCosts = maxPrice + refurb + sellingCosts + otherCosts + buyersPremium (solve algebraically)
  Also enforce: GDV - maxPrice - allCosts ≥ minimumProfit
  maxPurchasePrice = minimum of both constraints

BRRR:
  From 75% rule: maxPrice = (0.75 × postRefurbValue) - refurb - otherCosts - leaseExtensionCost - buyersPremium
  From cash left in: maxPrice = postRefurbValue × 0.75 - refurb - otherCosts - targetCashLeftIn
  maxPurchasePrice = minimum of both constraints

SA:
  Same iterative approach as BTL using SA income formula (nightly × 30.4167 × occupancy/100)

R2R:
  maxLandlordRent = monthlyIncome - targetMonthlyProfit - monthlyOpCosts
  Display as "Maximum rent to landlord: £X/mo" — not a purchase price
  Also show: maxSetupCosts = (annualProfit / (targetROI/100))
