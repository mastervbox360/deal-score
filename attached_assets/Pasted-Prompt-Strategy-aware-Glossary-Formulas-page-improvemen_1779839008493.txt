UNIVERSAL terms — show on ALL strategies (right column, Mortgage / Tax / Structure section):

Deal Score — Weighted composite score from 1 to 10 across six dimensions: Gross Yield, Net Cash Flow, Capital Growth Potential, Tenant Demand, Deal Structure, and Risk Profile
RECOMMENDED — Deal meets or exceeds all key thresholds for this strategy. Strong candidate for investor presentation
REVIEW — Deal partially meets thresholds. Worth progressing with negotiation or cost review
AVOID — Deal falls below minimum thresholds. Not recommended at current numbers
Sourcing Fee — Fee payable to the deal sourcer on legal completion
LTT / SDLT — Land Transaction Tax (Wales) or Stamp Duty Land Tax (England & NI). Government purchase tax on property transactions


BTL — Left column:
Section: Yield & Return

Gross Yield — Annual rent as a percentage of purchase price
Net Yield — Annual income minus operating costs divided by purchase price. Mortgage excluded (UK standard)
Cash-on-Cash ROI — Annual cash flow as a percentage of total cash invested. Includes mortgage payment
Payback Period — Years to recover total cash invested from annual cash flow
Equity on Day One — Immediate equity created when a property is purchased below market value
BMV — Below Market Value. The discount between purchase price and estimated market value

Section: Cash Flow & Expenses (marginTop: 10)

Gross Rent — Total rental income before any deductions or void allowance
Void Allowance — Estimated cost of vacancy. A 5% void allowance equals approximately 2.5 weeks vacant per year
Effective Rent — Gross rent after void allowance deducted
Net Operating Income (NOI) — Effective rent minus all operating costs, excluding mortgage
Net Cash Flow — Monthly surplus after all costs including mortgage payment
Break-Even Rent — Minimum rent required to cover all costs at the current mortgage rate
Rent Headroom — The buffer between contracted rent and break-even rent

BTL — Right column:
Section: Stress Testing & Resilience

Stress Test — Analysis of deal performance under a higher mortgage rate (+1.5%) and lower rent (−10%), applied independently
Break-Even Rate — The mortgage rate at which monthly cash flow reaches exactly zero
Sensitivity Analysis — Shows the impact on cash flow and ROI of a 10% rent reduction and a 1.5% rate increase independently

Section: Mortgage, Tax & Structure (marginTop: 10)

Interest-Only Mortgage — Monthly payment covers interest only. Capital balance remains unchanged throughout the term
Repayment Mortgage — Monthly payment covers both interest and capital. Balance reduces to zero over the term
LTV — Loan to Value. Mortgage amount expressed as a percentage of property value
Cash Invested — Total capital deployed: deposit + tax + refurb + other costs
Plus universal terms (Deal Score, RECOMMENDED/REVIEW/AVOID, Sourcing Fee, LTT/SDLT)


HMO — same as BTL plus this additional section in the right column after Mortgage / Tax / Structure:
Section: HMO (marginTop: 10)

Profit Per Room — Monthly cash flow divided equally across all rooms. Key HMO efficiency metric
HMO Licence — Required local authority licence for larger HMOs. Cost is a one-off acquisition cost, not monthly
Gross Monthly Rent — Total room rental income across all rooms at current occupancy rate

Net Cash Flow — Monthly surplus after all costs including mortgage payment

BRRR — Left column:
Section: Yield & Return

BRRR Gross Yield — Yield calculated on post-refurb value (GDV), not purchase price
Net Yield — Annual income minus operating costs divided by post-refurb value. Mortgage excluded
Cash-on-Cash ROI — Annual cash flow as a percentage of cash left in the deal after refinance
Equity Created — The uplift in property value above total cost in, realised through refurbishment
Money Out / ∞ Recycled — When the refinance loan exceeds total cost in. All capital returned plus a surplus. Cash-on-cash ROI is infinite

Section: Cash Flow & Expenses (marginTop: 10)

Gross Rent — Total rental income before any deductions or void allowance
Void Allowance — Estimated cost of vacancy. A 5% void allowance equals approximately 2.5 weeks vacant per year
Effective Rent — Gross rent after void allowance deducted
Net Operating Income (NOI) — Effective rent minus all operating costs, excluding mortgage
Net Cash Flow — Monthly surplus after all costs including the refinance mortgage payment

BRRR — Right column:
Section: BRRR Structure

GDV (Gross Development Value) — The estimated market value of the property after refurbishment is complete
Refinance Loan — Capital released by remortgaging against the post-refurb value at a set LTV
Cash Left In — Capital remaining in the deal after subtracting the refinance loan from total cost in
Total Cost In — All capital deployed into the BRRR: purchase price + tax + refurb + bridging interest + other costs
Bridging Finance — Short-term property-secured loan used to fund acquisition and refurbishment before refinancing

Section: Stress Testing & Resilience (marginTop: 10)

Stress Test — Analysis of deal performance under a higher mortgage rate (+1.5%) and lower rent (−10%), applied independently
Break-Even Rate — The mortgage rate at which monthly cash flow reaches exactly zero
Sensitivity Analysis — Shows the impact on cash flow and ROI of a 10% rent reduction and a 1.5% rate increase independently

Section: Tax & Structure (marginTop: 10)

LTV — Loan to Value. Mortgage amount expressed as a percentage of property value
LTT / SDLT — Land Transaction Tax (Wales) or Stamp Duty Land Tax (England & NI)
Cash Invested — Total capital deployed into the deal before refinance
Plus universal terms (Deal Score, RECOMMENDED/REVIEW/AVOID, Sourcing Fee)


FLIP — Left column:
Section: Project Returns

Total ROI — Net profit as a percentage of total project cost
Annualised ROI — Total ROI scaled to a 12-month equivalent based on project length in months
Profit on Cost — Net profit expressed as a percentage of total costs. Benchmark: 18%+ with planning permission, 25%+ without
Net Profit — Sale proceeds minus all acquisition, build, and selling costs
GDV (Gross Development Value) — The target sale price or estimated open market value after works are complete

Section: Project Costs (marginTop: 10)

Total Cost In — All capital deployed: purchase price + tax + refurb + contingency + holding costs + other costs
Holding Costs — Monthly financing or carrying costs during the project period
Selling Costs — Agent fees and legal costs on sale, expressed as a percentage of GDV
Contingency — A percentage buffer added to refurb costs to absorb unexpected expenses. DealScore default: 10%
Bridging Finance — Short-term property-secured loan used to fund acquisition and/or refurbishment

FLIP — Right column:
Section: Stress & Sensitivity

Cost Overrun Risk — Impact on profit if build costs increase beyond budget. A 10% overrun on a £30k refurb reduces profit by £3,000
GDV Sensitivity — Impact on profit if the achieved sale price is lower than the GDV estimate
Break-Even GDV — The minimum sale price at which the project still makes a profit

Section: Tax & Structure (marginTop: 10)

LTT / SDLT — Land Transaction Tax (Wales) or Stamp Duty Land Tax (England & NI)
Cash Invested — Total capital deployed into the project
Plus universal terms (Deal Score, RECOMMENDED/REVIEW/AVOID, Sourcing Fee)


R2R — Left column:
Section: R2R Income

Monthly Spread — The difference between sub-let income collected and rent paid to the landlord. The core R2R profit driver
Gross Monthly Income — Total sub-let rent collected from all rooms at current occupancy
Net Monthly Profit — Monthly income after rent to landlord, management fees, and running costs
Annual Profit — Net monthly profit multiplied by 12
Setup Costs — One-off costs to establish the R2R agreement: furniture, deposits, compliance, and fit-out

Section: Returns (marginTop: 10)

ROI on Setup — Annual profit as a percentage of total setup costs
Payback Period — Number of months to recover setup costs from monthly profit
Total Upfront — All capital needed to begin: setup costs + landlord deposit + first month rent

R2R — Right column:
Section: Agreement & Risk

Landlord Agreement — The lease or licence between the sourcer and the property owner permitting sub-letting. Term, break clauses, and subletting rights must be clearly documented
Subletting — Renting out a property or individual rooms that you yourself rent from the owner
Occupancy Rate — Percentage of rooms occupied and generating income. DealScore default: 90%
Management Fees — Platform or agent commission charged on gross income collected

Section: Structure (marginTop: 10)

Cash Invested — Total setup costs deployed to establish the agreement
Plus universal terms (Deal Score, RECOMMENDED/REVIEW/AVOID, Sourcing Fee, LTT/SDLT)
